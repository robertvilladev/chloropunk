# Chloropunk — SFX (ZzFX) Plan

**Goal:** Cyberpunk ZzFX sound effects on about 10 game events, plus an SFX volume setting. Spec: [audio addendum](../specs/2026-10-23-audio-addendum.md).

**Tech:** ZzFX's sample generator vendored as `src/fx/zzfx.ts`, because the npm `zzfx` module creates its own AudioContext on import. Samples play through Phaser's WebAudio context.

## Concepts (first time doing game audio)

- **Shared AudioContext.** Browsers give a page one audio "engine" (`AudioContext`). Phaser already makes one, and it handles the **autoplay unlock**: browsers keep audio suspended until the first click or keypress, and Phaser resumes it then. We render ZzFX samples into buffers and play them on Phaser's context, into Phaser's master gain. The unlock, mute and blur suspend then cover SFX and future music alike. Sounds asked for while the context is still locked are dropped, not queued, so the first click doesn't burst out a backlog.
- **Voice limiting.** A "voice" is one playing copy of a sound. Each sound gets `max` concurrent voices and a `gapMs` minimum between starts (`src/core/voices.ts`). A hit can't stack 30 times on one frame.
- **Pitch/volume variation.** Each play is detuned by a random ±6% and its volume varies ±10%. Repeats then sound organic. Scrap pickups also step their pitch up on a quick streak (the classic coin-chain rise).
- **Bitcrush / glitch.** ZzFX's `bitCrush` holds samples (lo-fi), `noise` and `modulation` add grit, and square/saw shapes plus pitch jumps give the digital "blip" character.
