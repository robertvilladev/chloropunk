**Goal:** Wave 10 is a boss fight against a big humanoid made of the five vegetables, with three attack patterns plus pea support spawns, no timer, ending in the run summary.

**Architecture:** The boss is data (`assets/data/enemies.json` entry `boss`, behaviour `"boss"`) plus a pure state machine `src/entities/bossBrain.ts` that decides pattern, telegraph and phase from time, HP and player position; `BossManager` (or a boss branch inside `EnemyManager`, builder's call) turns its decisions into bolts, a charge, tomato blasts (reusing the exploder blast) and pea spawns. Wave 10 in `waves.json` gets `"boss": true` and a reduced-rate pea schedule; `GameScene` skips the timer for a boss wave and ends the wave on boss death.

**Tech Stack:** Phaser 3.90, TypeScript strict, Vitest.

**Spec:** [arena map addendum](../specs/2026-10-23-arena-map-addendum.md) §3 (boss design); MVP spec §4 step 2 (wave 10: no timer, reduced-rate support spawns), §5 enemies (boss with 2–3 patterns). Needs: [A5](2026-10-23-arena-5-obstacles.md) merged (charge stops on obstacles), the boss art approved in the [art refresh](2026-10-23-art-refresh.md) Phase A (placeholder texture allowed until Phase B lands), [M4a](2026-10-23-m4-run-end.md) for the victory hand-off.

## Global Constraints

- Patterns (addendum §3): **carrot volley** (5–7 aimed bolts, fan), **pepper charge** (0.6 s telegraph line, straight dash, stops on obstacles/arena edge), **tomato barrage** (3–5 ground markers, exploder-style blasts), **pea brood** support spawns. Phase 2 below 50% HP: shorter cooldowns, two patterns may overlap.
- Every attack is telegraphed ≥ 0.5 s; telegraph colour = danger; readable with reduced shake on.
- Boss spawns at a known landmark (helipad, `arena.json`), with an intro banner ("COMPOST COLOSSUS").
- Boss HP bar in `UIScene` (top centre, replaces the timer on the boss wave).
- Boss deterministic per seed (`createRng` stream of its own, like `combatRng`).
- Pierce/knockback: boss is immune to knockback; bolts/blasts reuse pooled objects.
- Balance: sim gets a boss row (HP vs. the reference builds' DPS); target: `typical` build wins wave 10 with 20–60% HP left in the sim, `never` build loses.

## Review Focus

1. Boss dies during a charge or with tomatoes in the air: pending attacks clear, wave ends once, summary shows victory once.
2. Player dies while the boss is at 1 HP / simultaneous kill: exactly one outcome (defeat wins if HP ≤ 0 first in the frame; test the order).
3. Pause mid-telegraph: timers freeze and resume (scene timers/tweens, not wall clock).
4. Boss stuck on an obstacle during a charge or walking: charge stops; walking uses A5 avoidance.
5. `?wave=10&build=…` dev start works for every reference build.

## Outcome (2026-10-26)

Shipped on `feat/m4b-boss`. All four tasks done; owner playtest (Task 4 step 2) still open.

- **Brain** `src/entities/bossBrain.ts` (pure, 100% covered): `bossStep` picks volley / charge / barrage (never the same twice in a row) from its own seed stream (`seed + wave + 104729`), telegraphs, fires with the telegraph's aim, then stays busy for `recover`; phase 1 one attack at a time, phase 2 (below 50% HP) up to two with `gap` 1.6 → 0.9 s; pea brood on its own cadence (5 s → 3.5 s, 3 peas). `bossPose` picks the frame (attack pose > hit > idle, `_p2` in phase 2, phase-1 `hit` for the 120 ms switch flash). `bossParams` reads the flat `enemies.json` `boss.params`.
- **Game** `src/managers/BossManager.ts`: the boss is one sprite added to the enemy group (weapons, contact, `clearAll` and the autopilot see it; `EnemyManager` skips steering it, knockback immune). Volley = pooled enemy bolts with the look's art (`BOSS_ART`, rotated), charge = 520 px/s dash for ≤ 1.2 s that stops on a vent or wall, barrage = lobbed shots flying over the telegraph to danger markers, then `Juice.blast` + the exploder's damage path; brood = `EnemyManager.telegraphAt` behind the boss. Telegraphs: one Graphics in the danger colour (fan lines, charge lane, markers), fading in. Everything runs on the scaled frame dt, so pause and dev speed apply and a boss death mid-barrage drops the lobs (`clear`). Spawns at the helipad; intro banner = the look's name; HUD swaps the timer for name + HP bar (pink in phase 2); BOSS DOWN banner, then the existing Victory → Summary victory.
- **Wave flow:** `waves.json` wave 10 = `{ boss: true, groups: [] }`; `isBossWave`, `waveDuration` = Infinity (tested); `GameScene` tracks `elapsed` (run log duration); the boss's death ends the wave after the frame's systems run, so a player death earlier in the same frame wins (exactly one outcome).
- **Numbers** (builder's call, sim-checked): HP 6500, bolt 6 ×5–7, charge 14, blast 10 (r 60), contact 12. **Sim:** `simulateWave` models the boss as one big foe (guns clear the brood first) plus its attacks as a damage rate (`bossHit` 0.3 land); `bossTargets` in `checkTargets`: typical kills it in ~53 s with 29% HP left (target 20–60%).

**Deviations from this plan:**
- Banner/name per look (`BOSS_NAMES`), not "COMPOST COLOSSUS" (look dropped 2026-10-24).
- The boss holds 200 px from the player instead of walking into them: walking contact is untelegraphed, and in the first autopilot runs it dealt most of the damage (spec: every attack telegraphed). Contact only hurts during a charge or if the player walks into it.
- The boss loads in `GameScene.preload` of wave 10, not during the wave-9 shop (~190 ms at that wave's start; move it to `ShopScene` if the hitch shows).
- No `pnpm runs` boss rows and no run-log test for the boss wave: the existing wave record covers it (outcome, `duration` = elapsed).
- `scripts/playtest.mjs` waited for the removed `GameOverScene`; it now stops on `SummaryScene` (stale since M4a).

**Verified:** `pnpm test:coverage` (326 tests), `pnpm build`. Headless Chromium (Playwright, swiftshader WebGL and Canvas), `?wave=10&build=typical`: all three looks (seed 1 Harvester-9 WebGL, 2 Onion King WebGL, 3 Onion King Canvas, 6 Greenhouse Canvas): intro banner, each telegraph paused mid-wind-up (Review Focus 3: pause freezes and resumes the telegraph), phase-2 switch, kill → BOSS DOWN → Victory, run saved `won`, no console errors. Autopilot (never dodges a telegraph) at `?wave=10&build=typical`: dies in 35 s with the boss at ~17% HP (5419 of 6500 dealt), mostly to charges it runs along. Standing still: dies in 33 s.

**Carry-overs:** owner playtest of difficulty and readability; the sim's `bossHit` is a guess until boss logs exist; the autopilot does not dodge boss telegraphs, so `pnpm playtest` runs end at the boss; volley lines start at the sprite centre, not the gun.
