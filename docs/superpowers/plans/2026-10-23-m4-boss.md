# M4b — Boss (Compost Colossus) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Wave 10 is a boss fight against a big humanoid made of the five vegetables, with three attack patterns plus pea support spawns, no timer, ending in the run summary.

**Architecture:** The boss is data (`assets/data/enemies.json` entry `boss`, behaviour `"boss"`) plus a pure state machine `src/entities/bossBrain.ts` that decides pattern, telegraph and phase from time, HP and player position; `BossManager` (or a boss branch inside `EnemyManager`, builder's call) turns its decisions into bolts, a charge, tomato blasts (reusing the exploder blast) and pea spawns. Wave 10 in `waves.json` gets `"boss": true` and a reduced-rate pea schedule; `GameScene` skips the timer for a boss wave and ends the wave on boss death.

**Tech Stack:** Phaser 3.90, TypeScript strict, Vitest.

**Spec:** [arena map addendum](../specs/2026-10-23-arena-map-addendum.md) §3 (boss design); MVP spec §4 step 2 (wave 10: no timer, reduced-rate support spawns), §5 enemies (boss with 2–3 patterns). Needs: [A5](2026-10-23-arena-5-obstacles.md) merged (charge stops on obstacles), the boss art approved in the [art refresh](2026-10-23-art-refresh.md) Phase A (placeholder texture allowed until Phase B lands), [M4a](2026-10-23-m4-run-end.md) for the victory hand-off.

## Global Constraints

- Patterns (addendum §3): **carrot volley** (5–7 aimed bolts, fan), **pepper charge** (0.6 s telegraph line, straight dash, stops on obstacles/arena edge), **tomato barrage** (3–5 ground markers, exploder-style blasts), **pea brood** support spawns. Phase 2 below 50% HP: shorter cooldowns, two patterns may overlap.
- Every attack is telegraphed ≥ 0.5 s; telegraph colour = danger; readable with reduced shake on.
- Boss spawns at a known landmark (helipad, `arena.json`), with an intro banner ("COMPOST COLOSSUS").
- Boss HP bar in `UIScene` (top centre, replaces the timer on the boss wave).
- Boss deterministic per seed (`createRng` stream of its own, like `combatRng`).
- Pierce/knockback: boss is immune to knockback; bolts/blasts reuse pooled objects.
- Balance: sim gets a boss row (HP vs. the reference builds' DPS); target: `typical` build wins wave 10 with 20–60% HP left in the sim, `never` build loses.

## Review Focus

1. Boss dies during a charge or with tomatoes in the air: pending attacks clear, wave ends once, summary shows victory once.
2. Player dies while the boss is at 1 HP / simultaneous kill: exactly one outcome (defeat wins if HP ≤ 0 first in the frame; test the order).
3. Pause mid-telegraph: timers freeze and resume (scene timers/tweens, not wall clock).
4. Boss stuck on an obstacle during a charge or walking: charge stops; walking uses A5 avoidance.
5. `?wave=10&build=…` dev start works for every reference build.
